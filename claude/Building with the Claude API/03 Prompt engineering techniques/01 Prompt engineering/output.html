# One-Day Carbohydrate Loading Meal Plan for Marathon Training

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete preparing for marathon race day with carbohydrate loading:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (120g), Fat (85g), Carbs (480g)

**Meal Plan:**

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with honey (30g), sliced banana (120g), and whole milk (240g). 1 whole wheat toast (40g) with peanut butter (20g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Sports drink (500ml) with 6% carbohydrates. Granola bar (50g).
    * Protein: 5g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 75g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Grilled chicken breast (130g) with pasta (120g dry weight cooked in water) topped with marinara sauce (100g). Side salad with mixed greens (150g) and olive oil dressing (15g).
    * Protein: 42g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 105g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Rice cakes (60g) with jam (40g). Orange juice (250ml).
    * Protein: 4g, Fat: 2g, Carbs: 95g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Lean ground turkey (140g) with white rice (150g dry weight) and steamed carrots (150g). Low-fat yogurt (170g) on the side.
    * Protein: 40g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 95g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Bagel (90g) with honey (20g) and banana (100g).
    * Protein: 11g, Fat: 3g, Carbs: 75g

This meal plan emphasizes high-quality carbohydrates with moderate protein to support glycogen storage while maintaining muscle integrity during intense marathon training. The timing of meals and snacks supports sustained energy levels leading up to race day.
